**PSXS5 2.1.0**: themes, light guns, adaptive triggers, profiles, text guides, game art and a lot more.

## Looks

- **Five themes** (Settings → Library → Theme): Classic, Neon Arcade, Memory Card, Memory Card Dark and Record Shelf, each with its own colours, fonts and shelf. Memory Card lays the games out in a grid with a detail card; Record Shelf shows them as spines on a shelf. The in-game screens follow the theme.
- **Shaders for Beetle on the GPU** (Settings → Display → Shader): sharp bilinear and a CRT look (scanlines, RGB grille, glow).
- **Picture colours** (Settings → Display → Colour): brightness, and Vivid, Soft, Warm, Cool or Black and white.
- **Game artwork borders** (Settings → Display): the game's own artwork around a 4:3 picture, from [The Bezel Project](https://github.com/thebezelproject), downloaded the first time.
- **Beetle widescreen** when a game has no widescreen code, and **HD texture packs** for Beetle.

## Controller

- **The DualSense as a light gun** for GunCon and Justifier games (Time Crisis, Point Blank, Die Hard Trilogy...): point the controller at the screen to aim, R2 fires, L2 reloads, R3 re-centres. On automatically for known gun games (Settings → Controls → Light gun).
- **Adaptive triggers:** R2 firms up like a gas pedal in racing games and clicks like a trigger with a light gun.
- **Gas and brake on R2 / L2** in racing games where Cross is the gas and Square the brake (Gran Turismo, Ridge Racer, Colin McRae...).
- **Rumble feel:** Classic, Soft, Punchy, or Punchy in the triggers too.
- **Stick dead zone and response**, for drifting sticks or careful aiming.
- **Player order:** choose which controller is player 1, 2, 3 and 4.
- **Light bars:** player colours or the game's cover colour.

## Playing

- **Auto-save** (Settings → System, off unless you turn it on): every 5, 10 or 15 minutes into three slots; load them from the PSXS5 menu → Auto-saves.
- **Text guides:** put a walkthrough `.txt` next to a game (or in its `guides` folder) and read it from the PSXS5 menu → Guide. Monospace, so ASCII tables line up; your place is kept.
- **Manuals:** page images in `<game folder>/manual/`, read from the PSXS5 menu.
- **The PSXS5 menu shows the time** and how long you've been playing.
- **Overclock** (Settings → System): fewer slowdowns in games that slow down on a real console.
- **Screenshots** (touchpad + Square), **a speedrun timer** (touchpad + Triangle / Circle), **next disc** (touchpad + R1).
- **Find a code:** search the game's memory for a value (lives, money...) and turn it into your own cheat.
- Achievement pop-up styles (banner, compact, big trophy) and a progress tracker you can switch off.

## Shelf

- **Profiles** (Settings → System → Who's playing): each person keeps their own memory cards, save states, settings, play time, favourites and RetroAchievements sign-in. Your existing data is the main profile. Sign a profile in with `python tools/psxs5_sync.py ra-login --profile "<name>"`.
- **Title screen and gameplay pictures** in Details, downloaded with the covers.
- **RetroAchievements profile** (Settings → Achievements → Your profile, or Square in Your library): points, games mastered, recent unlocks, games closest to mastery. Covers show a trophy badge with your progress.
- **Idle slideshow:** after a minute without a button on the shelf, your games go by full screen.
- **Surprise me** (touchpad), **hide a game** (L3 in Details), **Your library** stats, per-game tips in Details.
- The phone page has a **Saves** tab (memory cards and save states: download, upload, slot pictures) and notes on save-state slots.

## Fixes

- Games whose `.cue` has no quotes around the file name, or whose `.bin` was renamed, now get their serial: region and cover download work again for them. Games still without one are named in the log.
- The shelf runs faster (about 10 ms a frame instead of 30).
- The shelf clock follows the console's time zone and 12/24-hour setting.
