The first release of **PSXS5 (PlayStation X Super 5)**, a PlayStation 1 emulator that runs as a native app on a jailbroken PS5.

## What's included

- **The PSXS5 app** (`PPSA97510`), built on PCSX-ReARMed with its built-in HLE BIOS.
- **Cover-flow shelf.** Covers matched by disc serial and downloaded on the console. Flat or 3D-box style, with interface sounds (five styles, or off).
- **Formats:** `.cue`/`.bin`, `.chd`, `.pbp`, `.iso`, `.img`, `.mdf`, `.ccd`, and `.m3u` playlists for multi-disc games.
- **Video:**
  - internal resolution native or 2x
  - upscale 1x to 4x with Sharp, Scale2x/3x or xBR
  - aspect ratios: auto, 4:3, 16:9, 16:10, 1:1 or stretch
  - integer scaling, smoothing, dithering, FPS counter
- **RetroAchievements:** sign in from your PC with the token only, unlock banners, leaderboards, hardcore mode.
- **Cheats:** RetroArch `.cht` support, automatic per-game matching, and toggles from the in-game menu.
- **Play features:** save states (10 slots), per-game memory cards, disc changing, reset.
- **DualSense:** analog with rumble, and the left stick as a D-pad on digital-only games.
- **PC tools** (`tools/psxs5_sync.py`): prepare and upload your library, then covers, cheats, BIOS, RetroAchievements sign-in and app updates.
- `PSXS5-symbols.txt`: turns crash addresses in the log into function names, for bug reports.

## What you need

- A jailbroken PS5 with etaHEN and kstuff (developed on firmware 13.60)
- ShadowMountPlus
- An FTP client, or the included PC tool
- **Your own games**, and optionally **your own BIOS dump**

> No BIOS, games or keys are included, and none will be provided.

## Install

1. Extract `PSXS5-v1.0.0.zip`.
2. Copy the `PPSA97510` folder to `/data/homebrew/` on the PS5, and set its permissions to `777` (recursively). Or run:
   ```bash
   python tools/psxs5_sync.py app --app-dir PPSA97510 --host <PS5 IP>
   ```
3. Put your games in `/data/PSXS5/games/<Game name>/`.
4. Optionally, put your BIOS in `/data/PSXS5/bios/`.
5. Restart ShadowMountPlus and start PSXS5 from the home screen.

The [README](https://github.com/SynoPiia/PSXS5#readme) has the controls, settings and every PC-tool command.

## Known issues

- Rendering is on the CPU: internal resolution goes up to 2x, and HD texture packs aren't supported.
- Closing the app with the PS button crashed the console once during testing, with `/data` unlocked through etaHEN. If it happens, set *Settings → System → Unlock /data with etaHEN* to Off.
